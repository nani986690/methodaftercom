JAN SHARP MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP

Jan Sharp Memorial Scholarship Fund

The Jan Sharp Memorial Scholarship Fund honors Jan Sharp, a former Board member who passed away while serving Method. We are continuing her vision for the Center. The fund also has its own Facebook page, which at one time promoted our community connections and civic service.
