OUR STORY

A Legacy Of Inclusion & Quality Care

Method Child Development Center began in 1969 as Wake County's first multi-racial child care center. Founded by five local churches, the Center was created to provide quality early childhood education for children from all socioeconomic backgrounds. Today, Method continues that mission as a nonprofit organization serving infants through school-age children in a welcoming and inclusive environment.

Founded In 1969

Established through the Raleigh Inter-Church Housing Corporation to provide affordable child care for families across the community.

Inclusive Community

Method has proudly welcomed children from diverse backgrounds for more than five decades while celebrating every family's uniqueness.

Five-Star Quality

The Center earned North Carolina's 5-Star License and continues to provide high-quality early childhood education in a safe, supportive environment.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission and vision are also shared in our Parent Handbook.

Our Mission

Method Child Development Center supports a diverse community of families by wrapping its arms around each child’s social, educational, and emotional needs within a safe, nurturing and affordable environment.

Our Vision

Method Child Development Center provides a daycare option for children of all backgrounds and circumstances that act as an extension and support to the family. Method achieves this goal by encouraging parents to join in the center community and taking part in center activities. We believe that working together, and celebrating the diversity of our community, we enrich our children’s lives with strong values and skills that will help prepare them for their future.

CENTER LEADERSHIP

Experienced Leadership, Caring Hearts

Our leadership team works closely with families and teachers to provide exceptional early childhood education. The Center is led by experienced professionals who are passionate about helping every child succeed while supporting families throughout their educational journey.

A Diverse & Tenured Staff

Our educators represent diverse cultures, countries, and languages from across the globe. Over 75% of our staff members have been with Method for more than 2 years, providing familiar, loving faces that children trust.

Classroom Staff & Low Ratios

Every classroom is staffed with caring educators maintaining low child–teacher ratios: 5:1 (Lambs), 6:1 (Koalas), 9:1 (Cheetahs & Lions), and 8:1 / 9:1 in Tigers / Wake 3s & NC Pre-K.

Dedicated On-Staff Cook

Unlike many childcare centers that serve catered or packaged food, Method has our own full-time on-staff cook preparing fresh, nutritious, and balanced meals and snacks on-site daily.

Continuous Professional Development

Teachers complete ongoing annual training to strengthen their knowledge and classroom practices.

CPR & First Aid Certified

All staff maintain current First Aid and CPR certifications and meet required safety standards.

Safe & Trusted Environment

Every employee completes background screening before joining our team, ensuring a safe environment for children.
